# Aethrix Launchpad

Build a premium modern agency landing page for "Aethrix Creatives" based on the uploaded deck and brand assets.

Aesthetic & Theme:
- Deep midnight navy palette (#070B1E / #0A0F2D) with luminous emerald-to-indigo gradient accents (#10B981 -> #06B6D4 -> #6366F1 -> #8B5CF6) and sleek glassmorphism matching the provided logos and hero banner.
- Display the Aethrix Creatives logo prominently in the navbar and footer, using the provided logo image files.

Key Sections:
1. Navbar: Brand logo, navigation links (Services, Workflow, Industries, Why Us, Contact), and "Start a Project" CTA button.
2. Hero Section:
   - Tagline: "Where Ideas Become Impact" with vibrant gradient text on "Impact".
   - Subheadline: "Creative Solutions for Modern Brands."
   - Pill badges: "Graphic Design", "UI/UX Design", "Web Development", "AI-Powered Creative".
   - Primary CTAs: "Start Your Project" (smooth-scrolls to the interactive form) and "Explore Services".
3. Core Services Matrix (interactive tabs / filterable grid):
   - UI/UX Design (Wireframing, Mobile & Web UI, Dashboards, Landing Pages, E-commerce)
   - Graphic Design & Branding (Logo Design, Brand Identity Guidelines, Pitch Decks, Corporate Reports, Marketing Collateral, Social Media Graphics)
   - Web Design & Development (Responsive Web Apps, Corporate & SaaS Websites, E-commerce, Speed & SEO Optimization)
   - AI-Powered Creative Solutions (AI Product Visuals & Mockups, AI Short-form Video/Reels, Image Enhancement, Creative Automation)
   - Canva Template Design (Custom Brand Kits, Social Media Kits, Presentation & Team Templates)
4. 3-Tier Workflow Framework:
   - Level 1: Agency Workflow (Discovery, Strategy, Production, Quality Check, Delivery)
   - Level 2: Service Flow (Tailored step-by-step pipeline per creative discipline)
   - Level 3: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs ensuring pixel-perfect consistency and reliable turnaround)
5. Target Industries & Audiences:
   - Interactive grid featuring Tech & SaaS, E-commerce & Retail, Startups & Corporate, Healthcare, Education, NGOs & Nonprofits.
6. Why Choose Aethrix:
   - The 6 value drivers from the presentation: Creative Meets Strategy, Tailored Design, Modern AI-Enhanced Workflow, Reliable Delivery, Consistent Brand Experience, Long-Term Creative Partner.
7. Interactive Scope Inquiry Form (Primary Conversion):
   - Multi-step interactive flow:
     * Step 1: Select service(s) needed (UI/UX, Graphic Design, Web Dev, AI Creative, Canva Templates)
     * Step 2: Project budget range and estimated timeline
     * Step 3: Contact details (Full Name, Work Email, Company/Brand Name, Project Brief)
     * Rich feedback state with clear submission confirmation.
8. Footer: Brand identity, full service directory, mission statement ("Where Ideas Become Impact"), social channels (LinkedIn, Behance, Dribbble, Instagram, X), and copyright.
